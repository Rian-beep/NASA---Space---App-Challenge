# Plain language

Lunar Window should make sense to someone who does not know space terms.

- Lead with everyday words. Put the technical name second, and only in the detailed view.
- Sunlight means the Sun is above the ground line. A clear view of Earth means Earth is above the ground line.
- A gap is a stretch of time when that view is blocked.
- Practice spots are places we chose for the demo. They are not official landing sites.
- The ice number is a computer model’s guess, not a measurement, and it does not change the sunlight or Earth-view results.
- Say whether the person’s chosen limits are met. Do not say a landing is safe or approved.
