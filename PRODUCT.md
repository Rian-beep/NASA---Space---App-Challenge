# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People learning about the Moon, and planners comparing a landing time, including a simpler Explore mode and a more detailed Plan and Analyze mode. Inferred from the existing app and the challenge brief. The user was not asked again in this session because no question tool was available.

## Product Purpose

Lunar Window shows whether a place on the Moon keeps sunlight and a clear view of Earth for a chosen arrival, using saved NASA sky and ground data for three practice spots near the south pole. It also shows the whole Moon and published spacecraft locations. It is not a flight check and does not approve a landing.

## Positioning

A plain-language planning view that keeps the sunlight check separate from a global Moon picture and from NASA’s list of past spacecraft.

## Constraints

- Do not call the result safe, approved, or flight-ready.
- The sunlight and Earth check runs only where saved sky and ground data exist: the three practice spots.
- Mission coordinates come from NASA’s NSSDCA spacecraft table. Sites with unknown locations are left out.
- The Moon picture is NASA LRO color (SVS 4720) with LOLA hill shading.
- Keep the words everyday-first.

## Brand

The name is Lunar Window.
